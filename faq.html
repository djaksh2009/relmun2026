RELMUN stands for Regional Engagement & Leadership Model United Nations. It is an online MUN conference focused on diplomacy, debate, negotiation and leadership.
RELMUN 2026 will take place on 26–27 December 2026.
Yes. RELMUN 2026 is an online conference.
Delegate registrations open on 1 October 2026.
The current registration plan is free. Any confirmed changes to fees will be announced by the RELMUN organising team.
RELMUN 2026 has six committees: UNSC, UNHRC, UNODC, AIPPM, UNW and IPLA.
From 1 October, delegates can choose between the available registration platforms listed on the official Registration Hub.
Certificates will be provided to eligible participants. Further certificate details will be communicated by the team.
