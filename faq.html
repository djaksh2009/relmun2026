Delegate Information / 01
RELMUN '26 is an online Model United Nations conference organised to bring together delegates from different backgrounds for debate, diplomacy, negotiation and collaborative problem-solving.
RELMUN '26 will take place online on 26–27 December 2026.
Yes. RELMUN '26 is a completely free conference and there is no registration fee for delegates.
Delegate registrations officially open on 1 October 2026. Registrations submitted before the opening date will not be accepted.
RELMUN '26 currently features the United Nations Security Council (UNSC), United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC), and United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC).
Delegates can enter their preferred countries or portfolios during registration. Final portfolio allotments are subject to availability and the decision of the Secretariat.
Yes. Delegates with no previous MUN experience are welcome to register. Training and relevant conference information will be provided to help delegates prepare.
Certificates will be provided to participants in accordance with the conference's certification structure.
Delegate registrations will be available through the official RELMUN registration process once registrations open on 1 October 2026.
For questions that are not answered here, please contact the RELMUN Secretariat through the official conference communication channels.
