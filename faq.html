RELMUN stands for Regional Engagement & Leadership Model United Nations. It is an online MUN conference built around diplomacy, debate, engagement and leadership.

RELMUN '26 takes place on 26–27 December 2026.

Yes. RELMUN '26 is an online Model United Nations conference.

Delegate registrations open on 9 October 2026.

Delegate registration for RELMUN '26 is ₹200 per delegate.

The six committees are UNSC, UNHRC, UNODC, AIPPM, UN Women and IPLA.

From 9 October 2026, delegates can choose from the available official registration platforms listed on the RELMUN registration page.

Certificates are provided to participants.
