01 / ABOUT RELMUN

WHERE DIPLOMACY BECOMES ACTION.

RELMUN '26 is a two-day online Model United Nations conference designed around engagement, leadership and strategic diplomacy.

Delegates will enter specialised committee environments where research, negotiation, collaboration and public speaking shape the course of debate.

The conference takes place on 26–27 December 2026.
