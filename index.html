RELMUN '26 is an online Model United Nations conference built around meaningful diplomacy, engagement and leadership.

Across two days, delegates will step into the roles of diplomats, policymakers and decision-makers to debate pressing issues, negotiate solutions and experience the dynamics of multilateral diplomacy.
