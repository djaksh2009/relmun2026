About RELMUN

A conference built around engagement.

RELMUN is designed to provide delegates with a platform to discuss international affairs, negotiate solutions and experience the dynamics of Model United Nations in an accessible online environment.

01

Debate

Engage with complex international issues and present arguments backed by research, diplomacy and policy.

02

Negotiate

Work with fellow delegates to build consensus, negotiate solutions and navigate the realities of international diplomacy.

03

Lead

Develop public speaking, diplomacy, collaboration and leadership skills through committee proceedings.
